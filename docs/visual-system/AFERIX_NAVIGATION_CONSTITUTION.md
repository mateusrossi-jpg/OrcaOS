# AFERIX — NAVIGATION CONSTITUTION

**STATUS: FROZEN | RATIFIED | DESIGN AUTHORITY**
**VERSION: 1.0**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## PART 1 — NAVIGATION PHILOSOPHY
**PURPOSE:** **C. EXECUTION**

Navigation in Aferix does not exist for exploration or discovery. It exists to move the technician between moments of execution as fast as possible. Every tap spent navigating is a tax on productivity.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## PART 2 — PRIMARY NAVIGATION MODEL
**MODEL:** **HYBRID (BOTTOM NAVIGATION + SIDEBAR)**

*   **Bottom Navigation:** Contains the 4 Pillars of Revenue (Home, Proposals, Work Orders, Finance). Optimized for immediate thumb reach.
*   **Sidebar:** Reserved for "Slow Tasks" (Settings, Catalog Management, Profile, Help).
*   **Command Center:** Tapping the Home icon while on Home opens a Quick-Search/Command bar.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## PART 3 — HOME AUTHORITY
The Home screen is the **Universal Anchor**.
*   **Startup:** App always opens on Home (Prioritization layer).
*   **Completion:** Completing a Proposal or Work Order returns the user to Home to re-prioritize.
*   **Recoverability:** Tapping the Logo (Top Center/Left) always returns to Home. No exceptions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## PART 4 — FAB CONSTITUTION (THE P0 ACTION)
*   **Official Action:** **[ + ] NEW PROPOSAL** (Lightning Composer).
*   **Position:** Bottom Right (Primary Thumb Zone).
*   **Scroll Behavior:** FAB hides on scroll down to maximize visibility; appears on scroll up.
*   **Contextual FAB:** In the Clients module, the FAB may switch to "New Client," but "New Proposal" must remain accessible via a long-press or secondary menu.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## PART 5 — BACK NAVIGATION
*   **Meaning:** "Back" always means "Return to previous context," never "Exit app."
*   **Rules:**
    *   **Android/iOS Swipe:** Must support native gestures.
    *   **Dead Ends:** No screen shall ever be a dead end. Every screen must have a "Back" or "Close" (X) visible in the top header.
    *   **Modals/Sheets:** Back gesture closes the top-most layer first.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## PART 6 — MODULE TRANSITIONS
*   **Home → Proposal:** Instant, zero-friction transition.
*   **Proposal → Work Order:** Triggered by event (Approval); transition should feel like a "Promotion" of the entity.
*   **Forbidden Friction:** Forcing the user to go through the "Client List" to start a Proposal is forbidden.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## PART 7 — MOBILE NAVIGATION LAWS
1.  **LAW #01:** 90% of navigation must be achievable with the thumb of the hand holding the device.
2.  **LAW #02:** Navigation items must never be smaller than 44x44px (Tap Target Minimum).
3.  **LAW #03:** "You are here" — The active module must be high-contrast and clearly identified.
4.  **LAW #04:** Breadcrumbs are forbidden. Use "Contextual Headers" instead.
