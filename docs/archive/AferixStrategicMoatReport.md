# AFERIX STRATEGIC MOAT REPORT (RC15)

This report defines the strategic foundation of Aferix and its competitive positioning in the Brazilian field service market.

---

### 1. WHY USERS BUY (The Trigger)
- **Professionalism**: Small service providers buy Aferix because their current Word/Excel proposals look "amateur." The **Executive PDF Engine** allows them to raise prices by signaling authority.
- **Speed**: The **Proposal Cart + Kits** reduces the "Proposal creation chore" from 10 minutes to 20 seconds.

### 2. WHY USERS STAY (The Moat)
- **The Customer Dossier**: Once 12 months of photos, voice notes, and payment history are in Aferix, leaving means losing the "brain" of the company. Rebuilding this relationship history in WhatsApp or Excel is impossible.
- **Commercial Memory**: Aferix remembers follow-ups and PMOC renewals that the human brain inevitably forgets.

### 3. WHY USERS RECOMMEND (The Viral Loop)
- **"The 20-Second Proposal"**: One technician showing another how they just closed a R$ 5.000 deal on the ladder via WhatsApp using Aferix is the primary referral engine.
- **Before & After Assets**: Automatically generated marketing cards that technicians share on Instagram.

### 4. WHY USERS LEAVE (The Churn Risk)
- **Onboarding Fatigue**: If the first 3 minutes feel like "admin work" (manual data entry), they quit.
- **Mitigation**: Smart CEP/CNPJ automation and pre-loaded Industrial Kits are critical.

---

### 5. TOP 3 KILLER FEATURES
1. **Voice-to-Dossier Execution**: Technicians *enjoy* using the app because they don't have to type. This captures data that was previously lost in chat.
2. **Next Money Radar**: A single view that prioritizes R$ 10.000+ opportunities, follow-ups, and collections.
3. **PMOC Shield**: Automates the entire recurring revenue cycle from visit to renewal.

### 6. THE STRATEGIC MOAT
**THE COMMERCIAL MEMORY LOOP**  
Aferix is the only system that connects **Field Evidence** (Voice/Photos) directly to **Commercial Action** (Upsells/Renewals) in a single loop. Competitors either do "Operations" (Field Service) or "Sales" (CRM). Aferix does **Revenue Intelligence** via relationship memory.
