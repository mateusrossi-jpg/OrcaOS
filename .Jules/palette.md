# Palette's UX & Accessibility Journal

## 2026-10-01 - Icon-Only Controls Accessibility & Focus Indicators
**Learning:** Micro-interactions like search clear buttons and toast close actions in mobile-first ERPs often lack explicit `type="button"`, `aria-label`, and `focus-visible` ring indicators, making keyboard navigation and screen reader feedback incomplete.
**Action:** Always provide explicit `type="button"`, concise localized `aria-label`s, and `focus-visible:ring-2` styles when creating or touching icon-only buttons.
